SLUDGE for macOS
================

Open Out Of Order.app to play Out Of Order. With OpenSLUDGE.app you can play
any SLUDGE game. When you open it, you choose the data file of the game.

Keyboard shortcuts
------------------
  Command+F or Alt+Enter   switch between a window and full screen
  Alt+A                    switch smoothing (antialiasing) on and off
  Control+click            right-click
  Command+Q                quit

Game controllers
----------------
  Left stick or d-pad      move the pointer
  A                        left click
  B                        right click
  Left or right shoulder   jump to the previous or next thing you can click on
  Start                    Escape (usually the game menu)

We keep saved games and settings in ~/Library/Application Support/SLUDGE.

If macOS says that the app can't be opened because it is from an
unidentified developer, or that it is damaged, Control-click the app,
choose Open, then choose Open again. On recent versions of macOS, you may
have to go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway"
instead.
